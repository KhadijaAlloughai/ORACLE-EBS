STEPS TO PLACE .FMX FILE IN EBS
================================

1. Compile the Form:
   KhadijaEmp.fmb -> KhadijaEmp.fmx

2. Open WinSCP.

3. Connect to the EBS Server.

4. Locate the compiled file:
   KhadijaEmp.fmx

5. Go to the EBS Forms directory:
   /u01/install/VISION/fs1/EBSapps/appl/fnd/12.0.0/forms/US

6. Copy KhadijaEmp.fmx to:
   /u01/install/VISION/fs1/EBSapps/appl/fnd/12.0.0/forms/US

7. SSH to the EBS machine.

8. Navigate to the Forms directory:
   cd /u01/install/VISION/fs1/EBSapps/appl/fnd/12.0.0/forms/US

9. Verify the file:
   ls -l KhadijaEmp.fmx
